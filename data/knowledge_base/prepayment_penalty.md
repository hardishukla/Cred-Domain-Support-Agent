# Prepayment Penalty

Prepayment and foreclosure charges vary significantly depending on the loan category and regulatory mandates. Personal loans incur a flat foreclosure charge of 3% on the outstanding principal balance. In compliance with RBI directives, Home loans under a floating interest rate regime carry nil prepayment penalties. Auto loans attract a 2% penalty if foreclosed within the first year, whereas Education loans have no prepayment charges at all, and Business loans carry a 4% fee on the outstanding amount.
