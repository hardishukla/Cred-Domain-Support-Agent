# Joint Accounts

Joint accounts provide convenient shared banking access and can support a maximum of 4 authorized account holders. These accounts predominantly operate under an 'any-or-survivor' mode, granting seamless transactional flexibility to all members. It is critical to note that all account holders hold equal and joint liability for any overdrafts or negative balances incurred. Individual KYC documentation is strictly required for each holder, and adding a nominee is mandatory during account opening.
