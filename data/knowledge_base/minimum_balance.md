# Minimum Balance

Account holders must maintain a specified Minimum Average Balance (MAB) based on their branch location. Standard Savings accounts require a MAB of ₹10,000 in metro areas and ₹5,000 in non-metro locations. A non-maintenance penalty of ₹500 per quarter is automatically deducted if the balance falls below the required threshold. Zero-balance accounts remain exclusively available for Jan Dhan Yojana participants, while premium NRI accounts stipulate a higher minimum balance of ₹25,000.
