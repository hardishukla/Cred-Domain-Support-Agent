# Loan Eligibility Criteria

Personal Loans require a minimum age of 21 and an annual income of ₹3,00,000 with a CIBIL score of 700 or above. Home Loans are available for individuals aged 24 to 65 with a minimum annual income of ₹5,00,000 and a CIBIL score of 750. Auto and Education loans require a minimum age of 18, with Education loans necessitating a co-applicant. Business loans require a minimum operational history of 3 years and a CIBIL score of 720.
