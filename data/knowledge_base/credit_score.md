# Credit Score

A robust credit score is essential for accessing premium financial products and securing favorable interest rates. Scores are calculated on a standard 300–900 range, with our institution strongly preferring profiles with scores of 750 or higher. Key impact factors include payment history which holds the highest weightage at 35%, followed closely by credit utilization at 30%. The length of credit history contributes 15%, while the credit mix and new credit inquiries evenly share the remaining 20% impact.
