# Credit Card Fees

Annual membership fees for credit cards range from ₹500 for entry-level cards to ₹5,000 for premium lifestyle tiers. Late payment charges are structured as a ₹500 flat fee plus a 3.5% monthly interest charge on the outstanding statement balance. All international transactions are subject to a 3.5% foreign currency markup fee. Reward points earned on card spends automatically expire after 24 months from the date of accumulation if unredeemed.
