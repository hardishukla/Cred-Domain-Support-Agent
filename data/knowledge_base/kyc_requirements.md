# KYC Requirements

A valid Permanent Account Number (PAN) card is mandatory for initiating any banking relationship. Customers must also provide an accepted address proof, such as an Aadhaar card, valid passport, or recent utility bill. As per regulatory guidelines, re-KYC must be completed every 2 years to ensure customer records remain updated. A secure Video KYC option is available for swift digital onboarding without visiting a branch.
