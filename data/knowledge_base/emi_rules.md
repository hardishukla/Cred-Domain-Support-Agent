# EMI Rules

Equated Monthly Installments (EMIs) are calculated using the standard reducing-balance formula to ensure interest is only charged on the outstanding principal. Repayment tenures are flexible, ranging from 12 months for small personal loans up to 360 months for large home loans. An auto-debit mandate is mandatory for all new loan accounts to facilitate seamless monthly deductions. A late EMI penalty of 2% per month on the overdue amount is levied for missed or bounced payments.
