# Interest Rates

Our lending portfolio features competitive interest rate slabs designed for various customer needs. Personal loans are offered at fixed rates ranging from 10.5% to 16.5% based on credit profile. Home loans feature attractive floating rates between 7.25% and 9.75%, linked to external benchmarks. Auto loans are available at 8.5% to 12.0%, while Education and Business loans are priced at 9.0%–11.5% and 12.0%–18.0% respectively, with options for both fixed and floating structures.
