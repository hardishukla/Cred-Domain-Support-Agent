# NRI Accounts

Non-Resident Indians have access to specialized account tiers tailored for cross-border financial management. The NRE account allows for fully repatriable and tax-free deposits of foreign income. Conversely, the NRO account is designated for managing Indian income, subject to local taxes, with restricted repatriation limits capped at ₹1 million per financial year. We also offer FCNR accounts for maintaining foreign currency deposits, requiring a valid passport and current visa for eligibility.
